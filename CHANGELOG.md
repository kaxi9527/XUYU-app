# 更新说明 · Changelog

## 0.9.9 预览版 · Preview

- 磁盘增长表支持每天定时采样、7–15 天历史、分类净变化、目录下钻及权限缺口说明。
- Disk growth adds daily scheduled samples, 7–15 days of local history, category changes, directory details and access reporting.

## 0.9.8 预览版 · Preview

- 品牌名称更新为「序屿 XUYU」，采用新的流线标志；现有数据和技术标识保持兼容。
- 首次使用说明默认访问范围；设置中可重新检查文件访问状态，已检查后发现无法访问时会提醒。
- 增加中英文安装、更新和贡献说明。

- Renamed the product to XUYU and introduced its flowing-line mark; existing data and technical identifiers remain compatible.
- The first-run flow explains the default access locations. Settings can recheck access, and a visible warning appears if a previously checked location becomes inaccessible.
- Added Chinese and English installation, update, and contribution guides.
